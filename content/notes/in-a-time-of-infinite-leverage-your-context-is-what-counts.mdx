---
title: In a time of infinite leverage, your context is what counts
date: 2026-07-18
---
In a time when leverage is growing with the tools at hand, your unique context and way of thinking through a problem are becoming even more pronounced. Routine work gets automated, and not thinking for yourself is, in my view, becoming more and more costly. When we all have AI and robots, distributions and options available in an abundance that never before existed, our own judgment and ability to think broadly and deeply become more valuable, not less, as the cost of execution goes down, taste, judgment, and your ability to imagine freely what could be built will be deeply valuable. You can have 1 person now building software and soon hardware through this form of leverage.&#x20;
