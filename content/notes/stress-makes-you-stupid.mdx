title: Stress makes you stupid
date: 2026-07-17
---
We know that we have fewer of our mental faculties available in a stressed or fearful state. \
\
Your best thinking is available in a peaceful mental state. \
\
Here are a few things you can do that lead to a calmer inner and outer environment. \
\
1 Put your phone away.\
2 turne of the news, at least for a day. \
3\. Stop engaging with the fear mongers.\
4 Make a list of all tasks that are open and circling in your head. \
5\. Isolate and name what you are stressed about. \
6\. Read, listen, and engage with people who are responding calmly and positively to challenges. \
7 once named, change how you frame the situations that introduce stress or fear. How could this be good? Is one of many questions that can change your inner stance towards a challenge. \
\
Taking care of your inner and outer environment is not only more enjoyable but also a strategic advantage.
