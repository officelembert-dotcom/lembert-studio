Power is a function of velocity, the rate at which one translates their intentions into reality.

Imagine a person who thinks I want to build this business or grow it to 100 million, and no second passes until they are already taking the first step to work on it.

There is just nothing in the way.

Now for most of us, this is not the reality of how we function. We will second-guess our own intentions, wonder if we are the right one to do it, justify it to ourselves, and if we can’t justify it, we will not do it.

We live in a world of reasons, conventions, and imagined rules which we are constantly trying to uphold. Assumptions about what we can and can’t do, who we can and can’t be, and our possibility is limited by our attachment to the reasonable mind.

So our power is diminished.

Or we are trapped in the world of time, in which everything seems to take a certain predetermined time. We look at what others did, what the conversation about time is in our field, and accept that to then reason against our own ideas.

To grow the business to this size takes 10 years, we might say. Or we say now is not a good time to start as we don’t have all the info yet, so we wait for the right time to do things.

If I look at the breakthroughs in performance I have facilitated, but also my own, they always came from a willingness to act now. To see now as the only time we have to take any action and risk getting it wrong.

Our mind is so attached to not making mistakes, to seeming great in the eyes of others, that we will always have reasons for why now is not a good time to act.

Yet the person of power acts, as he or she knows that the world only moves for the ones who do.

Where are you diminishing your own power by living in a world of reasons? What is an action you know you need to take but have been postponing, waiting for the right time?
