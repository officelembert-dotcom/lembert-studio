“Wow, I am not experiencing the world; I am experiencing my thoughts about the world...”

In a nutshell, this was going through my head while I stood in the middle of a club on New Year's Eve.

Prior to this moment, I had been experiencing what I would later call a crisis of meaning. I had been pursuing my career in dance for 8 years, but I had landed on the question of whether I wanted to continue. My relationship at the time was not working; I had been injured and felt deeply disconnected from life, constantly asking the question: What is this all for? Who am I? I dont know what I want. I dont know where to go.

This was running on autopilot in my mind. Mixed with questions about why I felt the way I did.

I had spent countless hours trying to find the why of my sense of disconnect, depressed feeling, and spent my time up in my thoughts without any relief up until this point.

When that thought came up, “I am experiencing my thoughts about the world,” it was as if I would suddenly sit next to my thinking and could watch it run. It became crystal clear why I felt the way I did; it was completely logical given how I had been thinking. It could not be otherwise.

A second thought occurred to me: “If I can think these thoughts, I can also think other thoughts and experience them.”

A rush of excitement flushed through my body as I felt a tremendous sense of possibility.

I could think anything!

This moment of insight changed my life, my career, and how I see human experience ever since.

I have seen countless people realize what their thinking has been doing to them and choose to think differently, and therefore get a different life.

From believing thoughts about the limit of their business to discovering that this was only what they thought, not a reflection of the potential of their business, to people dropping longstanding stories of suffering, disconnect, dissatisfaction, and more.

It opened up the possibility for me to not have to believe every thought that passes through my mind, to learn to see my feelings as an internal barometer for the quality of my thoughts, and to first look for insight before I assume the problem is with the other or out there.

If I could gift one thing to all humans, it would be a new relationship to their thoughts.

What about you? What becomes possible when you dont have to believe all of the thoughts you have each day?
